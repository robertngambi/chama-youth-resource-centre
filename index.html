We are Chama Youth Resource Centre


Chama Youth Resource Center provides quality vocational training certified by TEVETA and the Ministry of Youth, Sport and Arts. We empower young people with practical skills for employment and entrepreneurship.

We believe that talent is universal, but opportunity is not. Our mission is to bridge that gap by providing accessible, high-quality technical and vocational training in the district and the surrounding areas. Whether you are looking to become a master craftsman, a Fashion Designer, an IT specialist, a Bricklayer, a Carpenter or an Entrepreneur, we provide the tools and the mentorship you need to succeed.


Our Certifications:


We are a certified center under the Ministry of Youth, Sport and Arts, and proudly sponsored by TEVETA. Our programs are structured to take you from a beginner to a certified professional. Upon completion of our courses, you will receive a nationally recognized certificate and regional Trade Test (Level III, II, or I).

About Us


Chama Youth Resource Center is a certified skills training centre under the Ministry of Youth, Sport and Arts, located in Chama district, Eastern Province. We are the only recognised skills training centre in the circle of 164km. Currently, the institution runs four programmes: Bricklaying & Plastering, Carpentry & Joinery, Fashion, Design & Textile Technology, and ICT. We offer hands-on vocational training to empower youths with employable skills. Come One! Come All! at Chama Youth Resource, where you will learn lifelong survival skills, and contribute to the National Development of Zambia.


Motto

We are largely sponsored by TEVETA, and our graduates receive Trade Test Level III certificates after 3 months, with progression to Level II and Level I.

Our Services

At Chama Youth Resource Center, we offer a diverse range of skill programs designed to match your passion and the demands of the modern job market. Our programs are structured to cater to different levels of expertise, from beginners to those looking to advance their skills. We offer flexible duration options: 3-month intensive (Trade Test Level III), 6-month intermediate (Level II), and 1-year comprehensive (Level I).


Current Offerings (Available Now):


1. Carpentry and Joinery: From constructing door frames to building intricate furniture, our carpentry program provides students with fundamental skills for construction or starting a furniture business.


2. Cutting, Design, and Textile Technology (Fashion Design): This program teaches pattern making, garment construction, fabric science, and fashion design. Graduates become fashion designers, tailors, and textile entrepreneurs.


3. Bricklaying and Plastering: This course provides practical skills for construction, including brick laying, mortar mixing, plastering, and reading building plans. It is a direct pathway to employment in the construction sector.


4. Information Technology (ICT): Our ICT program covers computer basics, software applications (Microsoft Office), internet usage, and basic computer maintenance. This course equips students for administrative roles and further tech specialization.

Your Path to Certification:

· 6 Months (Level 2): Intermediate skills for supervisory roles.
